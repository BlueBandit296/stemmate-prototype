## How to view
Open the link in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a laptop or phone. No installation or account is needed.
Connectivity is simulated: use **Simulate offline / Reconnect** in the status bar.
All content is synthetic (fictional activities and one demonstration account, Facilitator A). Do not enter real names.
